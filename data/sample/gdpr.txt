The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) has applied across the European Union since 25 May 2018. Organisations must notify the supervisory authority of a personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Individuals have the right to erasure, often called the right to be forgotten, and the right to data portability. A Data Protection Officer must be appointed when core activities involve large-scale monitoring of individuals. The most serious infringements can lead to fines of up to 20 million euros or 4 percent of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
