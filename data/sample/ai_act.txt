The EU AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024 and regulates artificial intelligence using a risk-based approach. Systems fall into four tiers: unacceptable risk, high risk, limited risk and minimal risk. Practices with unacceptable risk, such as social scoring by public authorities, are prohibited. High-risk systems, for example those used in hiring or credit scoring, must meet requirements on data quality, documentation, human oversight and robustness. Limited-risk systems such as chatbots must tell users they are interacting with an AI. Penalties for prohibited practices can reach 35 million euros or 7 percent of worldwide annual turnover.
